Drop That Due

Never miss an expiry date again.

Drop That Due is a completely offline expiry management tool, designed exclusively for iPhone and iPad. All your data stays on your device — no internet, no account, ultimate privacy.

Key Features

  • 📅 Add items with expiry dates
  • ⏰ Local notifications (even when the app is closed)
  • 🔢 Home screen badge shows expiring count
  • 📂 Custom categories (Food, Medicine, Documents, etc.)
  • 📆 One‑tap import to system Calendar
  • 📤 Export data to CSV file
  • 🌐 Multi‑language support (Chinese, English, Japanese, Korean, Spanish)

Privacy Commitment

This app does not collect, upload, or share any user data. Everything works offline, with no internet permission required.
